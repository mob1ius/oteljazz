# Summary
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Not permitted: collection of this site's contents into datasets used to train
#                generative models.
# Permitted:     search indexing, and retrieval to answer a question a person has
#                actually asked, provided the source is cited and linked.
#
# This is a research site. Its text, audio, code, and figures accompany a manuscript under peer
# review and carry specific citation obligations, and the audio is itself redistributed under
# CC BY 3.0 with attribution as a licence condition. Ingestion into a training corpus strips the
# attribution that the licence requires be carried, which is the substantive objection here rather
# than a general one about machine reading.
