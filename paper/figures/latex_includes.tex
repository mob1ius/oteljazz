\caption{The ensemble's perceptual structure. Seven chord voices share a timbre, an onset grid,
and voice-led motion, so the cues that would segregate them into separate streams are deliberately
withheld and they fuse into a single harmonic object. The solo line and the walking bass remain
segregable because each violates those cues. The listener's load is approximately three concurrent
auditory objects rather than nine streams, and the oversight signals are properties of the fused
object: its thickness, its spelling, and its tuning.}
